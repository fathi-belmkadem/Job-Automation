
1. Baden-Württemberg (The "Länd")[1][2][3]
Website: startupbw.de
Why it's similar: This is the central portal for the entire state. Like Munich Startup, it features a huge map of the ecosystem, funding information, and an event calendar.
Also check: stuttgart-startups.de (specifically for Stuttgart).
2. Berlin
Website: deutsche-startups.de (Berlin Section)
Website: berlin-partner.de
Note: Berlin is so large it doesn't have one single "monolithic" portal like Munich. Deutsche-startups.de is the closest for news, while Berlin Partner is the official hub for resources and support.
3. Hamburg
Website: hamburg-startups.net[4]
Website: startupcity.hamburg
Why it's similar: Hamburg Startups is very close in "vibe" to Munich Startup—it is privately run, community-focused, and has a great news section. Startup City is the official government-backed portal.
4. North Rhine-Westphalia (NRW)[5][6][7]
Website: startups.nrw
Website: gruender.wiwo.de (Often covers NRW heavily due to location)
Why it's similar: Startups.nrw is a massive database and matching platform for the entire state (Cologne, Düsseldorf, Ruhr region).
5. Hesse (Frankfurt area)[3]
Website: starthub-hessen.de
Website: station-frankfurt.de
Why it's similar: Station Frankfurt is the go-to for news and events in the Frankfurt Rhein-Main area, very similar to the editorial style of Munich Startup.
6. Saxony (Leipzig/Dresden)[8][9]
Website: futuresax.de
Why it's similar: This is the state-owned platform for innovation and startups in Saxony, featuring competitions, networks, and alumni lists.
